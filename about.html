Our Story

REDs 3D Print Shop began as a passion project, fueled by a love for making and an obsession with quality. What started with a single printer has grown into a small but mighty workshop serving Peshtigo and the surrounding area.

We believe everyone should be able to bring their ideas to life. Whether you need a prototype for a big invention, a personalized gift for a friend or a unique decoration for your home, we’re here to help. Every piece is printed in‑house, hand finished and inspected to ensure it meets our high standards.

What We Stand For

At REDs we’re dedicated to quality prints, fair pricing and unmatched customer service. We treat every project like it’s our own, paying attention to the little details and working with you at every step.

  • Quality and precision
  • Passion for making
  • Community and collaboration
  • Hand‑finished details
  • Reasonable turnaround times

Our Process

  1. Consultation – We work with you to understand your idea and suggest materials, sizing and colours.
  2. Design & Prototype – We prepare your model and provide a preview if needed.
  3. Printing & Finishing – Your print is produced with precision and hand finished for a polished look.
  4. Pickup & Delivery – You collect your creation or we arrange local delivery on qualifying orders.
